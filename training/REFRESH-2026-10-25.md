## The comparison to run

Predeclared. Change none of it after a candidate is scored.

1. **Freeze the inputs.** The retrain starts on `develop` after the prerequisites below
   merge. Record its commit, `catalog_revision`, `routing_revision`, the compiled corpus
   sha256 from `training/data/corpus-lock.json`, and `python -m training.audit_catalog`
   (must show no document over the cap).
2. **Re-measure the baseline on that exact tree**, with the locked model, on each
   platform: `bench local --out`, `training.loss_inventory`, and the signoff set through
   `training.eval_two_stage`. Today's numbers above are not the comparison once the
   catalog moves.
3. **Train the recipe over three seeds.** One stage-1 retriever (seed 41), then stage 2
   at seeds 41, 42 and 43 with `--seed N --init-seed N` and the rest of `retrain.sh`'s
   arguments, each exported separately. Seed 41 is the one that ships; it is chosen now,
   not by score.
4. **Shortlist width.** k stays 32 unless measurement says otherwise. `bench scale` on the
   146-runbook catalog loses one required candidate at k=32 that k=48 recovers in
   retrieval alone; that says nothing about a head trained at 48. If the k=32 candidates
   still have a candidate miss after the prerequisites land, train one extra stage-2 arm
   at seed 41 with `--candidates 48`, and adopt it only if it clears every criterion
   below, removes the miss and keeps p90 inside the gate.
5. **Admission calibration.** Run `training/calibrate_floors.py` on the seed-41 candidate
   and apply its rule (the lowest floor that still buys recall). Report any floor it moves.
   The quality gate's floors in `benchmarks/runbook_selection/quality.py` do not change.
6. **Qualify**, every comparison same-runtime against step 2, int8, each platform on
   its own:
   - main suite: seed 41 and the seed mean each at or above the baseline's recall-pass;
     strict no more than 10 below the baseline (one seed's spread is about 7);
     `candidate_miss` 0, no forbidden load, docs at least 30/31, p90 inside the gate;
   - by shape: production-shaped (`bare`) losses no more than the baseline's, and each
     historical shape (active, history, docs, wake) no more than one worse;
   - the signoff set's criteria, as its README states them;
   - every untrained runbook in labels after export (`audit_catalog` lists none untrained);
   - report holdout rounds 1–6 beside these, but do not gate on them: they are development
     evidence.
7. **Decide.** Seed 41 qualifies on every line, or nothing ships. A failing seed 41 with a
   passing mean is reported, not replaced by a luckier seed. Then `selector_store.py
   publish`, the lock committed with the model and nothing else, and `/release` when the
   user says so.
